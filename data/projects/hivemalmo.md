## Overview

HIVE Malmö is a student-run startup organization that bridges the gap between university students and the local business community. I built the organization's web presence from scratch.

## My Role

- **Founder & Developer** — Created the organization and built its digital platform to connect students with industry partners.
- **Full Stack** — Developed the complete web application using SvelteKit on the frontend and Express.js with Firebase on the backend.
- **Community Building** — The platform served as a hub for events, member profiles, and partnership information.

## Technical Highlights

Built with SvelteKit for its lightweight, fast-loading pages — important for a student audience primarily accessing the site on mobile. Firebase handles authentication and real-time data, while Express.js powers the API layer for more complex operations like event management and member registration.
