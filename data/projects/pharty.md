## Overview

Pharty is a party photo gallery web app I built for my daughter's birthday. Guests could log in and upload candid photos during the celebration, which were instantly displayed on a shared photo wall — each accompanied by a personalized message from the birthday girl.

## My Role

- **Creator & Developer** — Conceived and built the entire application for a single, memorable event.
- **Real-time Gallery** — Photos appeared on the shared wall moments after upload, creating a live, evolving visual timeline of the party.
- **Personal Touches** — Each photo was paired with a custom message, adding a heartfelt layer to the gallery experience.

## Technical Highlights

Built with Next.js and TypeScript for rapid development, Shadcn UI for polished components, and Tailwind CSS for styling. The backend uses SQL for persistent storage of photos and messages, with optimized image handling to keep uploads fast even on mobile connections at the party venue.
